Persistent

Connection

The Persistent instance the flyout loads. Change this only if you host your own.

Signing in

You sign in inside the flyout itself, with the same email code, passkey or Google account you use everywhere else. This app stores no password and keeps no separate copy of your reminders.

Clearing removes this PC's saved session and offline copy, then reloads. Your account and reminders are untouched.

Alarms on Android work offline

Linux can keep reminders present and loop alarm audio while this PC is awake and Persistent is running. It catches up after reconnecting, but it cannot wake a sleeping or shut-down PC. Android can sound alarms without an internet connection.
